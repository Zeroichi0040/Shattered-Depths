# Shattered Depths

A small turn-based dungeon crawler that runs in the browser written in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

---

## Play it

**Live:** https://your-deployment-url-here

---

## How to play

| Key      | Action                                  |
|----------|-----------------------------------------|
| `W A S D`| Move one tile / attack in that direction|
| `Space`  | Wait (spends 1 AP, does nothing)        |
| `R`      | Restart, after death or after clearing  |

You attack by trying to move into an enemy. Bumping a wall is still an
action. The move fails but the AP is spent.
